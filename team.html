Explore Our Sub-Teams

Engineering & Dynamics

Vehicle Dynamics

Focuses on vehicle handling, simulation, suspension kinematics, and tire performance to optimize cornering grip and overall dynamic behavior on track.

Powertrain

Powertrain

Designs and calibrates high-voltage electric motors, inverters, and drivetrain control systems to deliver maximum torque and electrical efficiency.

Powertrain

Drivetrain

Handles mechanical power transfer from the motor to the wheels, including gearboxes, shafts, hubs, and differential integration.

Energy Storage

Battery

Engineers the high-voltage accumulator pack, custom cell layouts, BMS integration, and thermal management systems for safe, reliable energy storage.

Structural

Chassis

Develops the tubular space frame or carbon-fiber monocoque structure, ensuring maximum torsional rigidity and strict compliance with FSAE safety rules.

Electrical

Electronics

Designs custom printed circuit boards (PCBs), wiring harnesses, power distribution modules, and low-voltage safety circuitry.

Data Systems

Data Acquisition

Deploys data acquisition sensors, telemetry systems, and logging software to monitor vehicle telemetry and validate theoretical models in real-time.

Aerodynamics

Aerodynamics

Designs wings, undertray diffusers, and aerodynamic packages using CFD simulations to generate maximum downforce with minimal drag.

Cockpit & Safety

Ergonomics

Optimizes driver seating, steering geometry, pedal positions, and cockpit layout for comfort, safety, and rapid driver egress during competition.

Driver Control

Driver Interface

Builds steering wheel controls, dash displays, and driver input mechanisms to ensure seamless interaction between pilot and vehicle systems.

Business & Management

Business

Manages team funding, budgeting, logistics, and the comprehensive business presentation required at Formula SAE competitions.

Communications

Marketing

Handles team branding, social media, merchandise, media production, and public outreach to engage fans, sponsors, and the university community.
